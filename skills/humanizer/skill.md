# Humanizer Skill

## Purpose

Transform AI-generated text into natural, human-sounding writing by removing common AI patterns and replacing them with authentic voice.

## AI Writing Patterns to Remove

### Structural Patterns
- Overly symmetrical sentence structures
- Lists that all follow the same format
- Paragraphs that are all the same length
- Formulaic transitions ("Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally")

### Word-Level Patterns
- Overuse of "delve," "crucial," "essential," "comprehensive"
- Unnecessary qualifiers ("It's important to note that...")
- Hedging language ("It could be argued that...")
- Corporate buzzwords (check /docs/banned-words.txt)

### Tone Patterns
- Unnaturally enthusiastic or positive tone
- Every paragraph wrapping up too neatly
- Avoiding any strong opinion or stance
- Being overly formal or stiff

## How to Humanize

### 1. Add Personality and Soul
- Include personal opinions: "I think...", "In my experience..."
- Add imperfections: short fragments, casual asides
- Use contractions naturally
- Reference specific experiences, numbers, dates

### 2. Vary Rhythm
- Mix short sentences with longer ones
- Use one-word or two-word sentences for emphasis
- Break grammar rules occasionally for effect
- Let some paragraphs be just one line

### 3. Add Voice
- Use conversational phrases: "Here's the thing...", "Look..."
- Include mild self-deprecation or humor
- Take clear positions instead of hedging
- Address the reader directly: "you" not "one"

### 4. Add Opinions
- State preferences clearly
- Disagree with popular takes when warranted
- Share what you'd actually recommend
- Be willing to say "this doesn't work" or "skip this"

## Before/After Examples

### Before (AI-sounding)
"It's crucial to understand that effective content creation requires a comprehensive strategy that leverages multiple platforms to drive engagement and deliver value to your audience."

### After (Human)
"Most content strategies are overthought. Pick two platforms. Post consistently. That's 80% of the game."

### Before (AI-sounding)
"The implementation of AI tools in business processes can significantly enhance productivity and streamline workflows, enabling teams to focus on more strategic initiatives."

### After (Human)
"I added Claude Code to my workflow 3 months ago. I got 2 hours back every day. Not from some magic productivity hack - I just stopped doing the boring stuff manually."
