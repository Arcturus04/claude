# Best LinkedIn Post Examples

## Example 1: Tool Comparison
I stopped using AG last month.

Not because it's bad - it's genuinely impressive for quick prototyping.

But here's what I noticed after 3 months of daily use:
- I shipped faster but understood less
- My team couldn't maintain what it built
- Every "quick fix" created two new problems

Claude Code is slower at first. You have to think more. But 6 weeks in, my team actually owns the code.

The real question isn't which tool is faster. It's whether you want to outsource your thinking or sharpen it.

What's been your experience?

## Example 2: Practical Tip
The single biggest mistake I see with AI tools:

Starting with the code, not the plan.

I've watched 50+ developers use Claude Code. The ones who get 10x results all do the same thing - they spend 5 minutes in plan mode before writing a single line.

That's it. No fancy setup. No custom config. Just: think first, build second.

Try it tomorrow. Open Claude Code, hit Shift+Tab, and describe what you want before you ask it to build anything.

You'll be surprised how much time you save by "wasting" 5 minutes upfront.

## Example 3: Story-Based
Last Tuesday I had 12 LinkedIn posts to write.

Old way: 4 hours of staring at a blank screen, rewriting the same opening 6 times.

New way: Set up Claude Code with my brand voice, ran my slash command 4 times, reviewed and edited for 45 minutes.

Same quality. Maybe better - because I spent my energy on editing, not generating.

The shift isn't about AI replacing writers. It's about writers spending time on the part that actually matters: having something worth saying.
