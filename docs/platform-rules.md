# Platform-Specific Rules

## LinkedIn

- Max 3,000 characters per post
- Target 150-200 words for standard posts
- Start with a hook — first 2 lines must grab attention (they show before "see more")
- Use line breaks between every 1-2 sentences
- End with a question or CTA
- No hashtags (they reduce reach)
- No emojis in the first line
- Carousel posts: 8-12 slides, one idea per slide

## X (Twitter)

- Max 280 characters per post
- Threads: 3-7 tweets, each standalone but connected
- First tweet is the hook — make it punchy
- No hashtags unless absolutely necessary
- Use numbers and specifics ("3 months" not "a while")
- End threads with a recap or CTA

## Instagram

- Captions: 150-200 words for carousel posts
- Reels scripts: 30-60 seconds, conversational tone
- Start with a pattern interrupt
- Use line breaks after every sentence
- 3-5 relevant hashtags only (in a comment, not the caption)
- End with "Save this for later" or a question
