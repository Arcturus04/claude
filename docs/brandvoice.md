# Brand Voice Guide

## Personality

- Conversational but knowledgeable
- Confident without being arrogant
- Practical and results-oriented
- Slightly informal — contractions are fine

## Tone

- Talk to one person, not an audience
- Sound like a smart friend giving advice over coffee
- Be direct — get to the point fast
- Show, don't tell — use examples and stories

## Voice Patterns

### Do This
- "Here's what actually works..."
- "I tested this for 3 months and..."
- "Most people miss this, but..."
- "The real question is..."

### Don't Do This
- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
- "It is imperative that organizations..."
- "Leveraging synergies to drive outcomes..."
- "As a thought leader in the space..."

## Vocabulary

- Say "use" not "utilize" or "leverage"
- Say "help" not "empower" or "enable"
- Say "start" not "embark on" or "commence"
- Say "show" not "demonstrate" or "illustrate"
- Say "build" not "architect" or "engineer" (when used as verbs for general creation)

## Sentence Structure

- Lead with the insight, not the setup
- Keep sentences under 20 words when possible
- Use line breaks liberally — one idea per line
- Start with action verbs or bold claims

## Examples

### Good LinkedIn Post Opening
"I stopped using AG last month. Here's what happened to my workflow."

### Bad LinkedIn Post Opening
"In the ever-evolving landscape of AI-powered development tools, it's important to consider the implications of tool selection on organizational productivity."

### Good X Post
"Most AI tools help you code faster. Claude Code helps you think better. Big difference."

### Bad X Post
"Excited to share my insights on the transformative potential of AI-driven development methodologies! #AI #Innovation #Coding"
